# Switch vendor in the middle of a conversation

Owner said yes. From the T3 Code study (`02-agent-progress.md`, handover): the person changes the model or vendor on the
next message of an existing conversation. Faber hands the new vendor what happened so far, chosen by selection (the
messages, tool results and files that matter), not only a summary, and says on screen that the other vendor starts with
that handover. History stays in Faber's own conversation file, so it opens the same either way. Never reads the CLIs'
credential folders. Check R-numbers on "stays with their subscription" (Auto keeps history inside the CLI) and extend
rather than contradict. Words in every language; what's-new entry.
