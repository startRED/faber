# Words said twice, and the keys a desktop app has

From the 2026-10-22 sweep (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-panes-tabs-and-space.md`, section 6). Every language is
written by hand.

1. Placeholders that repeat their label or hold an example: the standing goal's field (`goals.placeholder` under
   `goals.words` and `goals.scope`) and the Ctrl+K box (`palette.label` and `palette.placeholder` both say search). Keep
   a short instruction only where the label does not already say it.
2. Ctrl+1, 2 and 3 for Chat, Projects and Catalog (outside the browser pane, where Ctrl+digits pick tabs), and Alt+Up and
   Alt+Down for the previous and next conversation in the sidebar's order, in the one `SHORTCUTS` table of `ui/keys.ts`
   so tooltips and the keys sheet follow; the Ctrl+K box holds them back while open.
3. The product map, a what's-new entry, and checks for the keys.
