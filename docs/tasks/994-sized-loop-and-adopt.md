Build slice 4 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-own-work-method.md` (approved as of commit 4fc1324d, R435). See
its sections "The loop, sized by risk", "Adopt before create" and "Faber's own development".

- **Size.** It is set at plan time. The engine raises it before a call runs, from the permission layer, when the call
  crosses a boundary or cannot be classified; the call waits for the contract.
- **Contract.** It goes in the plan: `Extends:` or `New:`, the proving check, and a target when the work is about speed,
  size or cost.
- **Receipt.** The engine writes it from recorded results, each tied to the hashes of the files it saw. A later change
  voids the result.
- **Review gate.** A fresh read-only session runs it, the other vendor when its quota allows, with the same-vendor
  fallback and "not reviewed" when none can start.
  - The blocking classes clear only by a fix or an answer that the reviewer or the person accepts.
  - Security takes two reviews, recorded separately.
- **Adapters.** Build them for Faber's R-numbered table, its taste cards and `docs/tasks/`, each with its own operations
  only. Prove them on Faber's own repo, with no second copy.
- **AGENTS.md.** Shrink "Done means" to its repo-specific lines.
- **Cost.** Measure the whole method's added cost on the token benchmark's scenarios.
