instead of holding it for a day") was open and not on `phase-1-base`. Its own checks were green; its last merge-queue

Verdict: not safe to queue as it is.

## Reclamation and ownership

- High, `scripts/cargo.mjs:102-108`: an hour-old reservation without an owner receipt is deleted without proof that
  its owner stopped. A live Node or native launcher can pause between creating the folder and publishing its receipt,
  and the native writer (`testkit/src/sweep.rs:152-177`, base) removes its receipt before its folder. After the
  deletion another claimant can enter, and the resumed writer can overwrite or remove the newer receipt. Older Node
  launchers have neither exclusive publication nor token checks. Elapsed time is not exclusion.
- High, `scripts/cargo.mjs:106-107`: a check-then-delete race even between new launchers. A reaper can see the folder
  empty, pause, then delete a receipt its live creator just published. `.faber-reap` excludes reapers, not publishers;
  the exclusive link at `:196` stops replacing a receipt, not deleting it.
- High, `scripts/cargo.mjs:112-120`: only the recorded launcher and its direct child are proved gone. A launcher and
  child killed from outside can leave a live grandchild. Cargo's lock does not cover arbitrary child work, private
  temporary files or the fingerprint deletion added later by #484.
- Windows, `:25-28`: reused PIDs and permission errors keep the reservation (safe, may block recovery). `:255-259`
  relies on `taskkill /T /F`; its failures keep uncertainty (`:285-293`). The rename retries (`:205-208`) handle sharing
  errors, not ownership races. Cleanup after a failed first publication (`:218`) removes the folder without checking
  its owner.
- Scope, `:30-40, 53-64`: temporary names are validated and linked components refused; no path traversal outside the
  target was found for stable paths. `:77` reads an orphan receipt before refusing a link (`:110` does it right).

## The empty ancestor of 2026-10-30

`:99, 128-155, 168-171` never reap an ancestor while acquiring a slot, and slot leases make a root acquisition refuse
before it reads the root's receipt. The empty root reservation with slot reservations below it, as on the owner's PC,
stays blocked: one waiting message, still cancelable. Keeping that unknown reservation is right; task 220 forbids
treating it as abandoned.
