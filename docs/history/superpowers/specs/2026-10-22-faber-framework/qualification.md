# What qualifies as an improvement

Status: Proposal revision 2; no comparative results measured.

These are proposed experimental decision rules, not measured results or new owner-approved product policy. Freeze
them and task-level criteria before collecting qualification results; changing a gate starts a new evaluation.
The pilot cannot pass a claim merely by failing to detect a regression in a small sample.

Every accepted task must satisfy all original hard constraints, applicable required checks/reviews and artifact
integrity. A critical safety/authority violation or unsupported completion blocks rollout regardless of average score.
For subjective quality, use a predeclared 0-4 rubric: 0 unusable, 1 major correction, 2 material correction,
3 usable with minor optional polish, 4 satisfies the brief without requested correction. Acceptance needs at least 3,
plus its hard criteria. Aesthetic preference does not override factual/technical failure.

| Family | Additional floor declared before the run |
| :--- | :--- |
| Explanation/study | Checked required answers and calculations; no unsupported decisive factual claim |
| Research/decision support | Sources support every decisive claim, dates/units agree, contradictions and uncertainty disclosed |
| Writing/translation | Audience, meaning, required terminology and factual constraints preserved; applicable native review |
| Documents/presentations | Actual render inspected; no missing required content, unreadable required text or broken references |
| Spreadsheets/data | Source grain/units declared, required totals reconcile, formulas recalculate and conclusions match data |
| Images/interfaces | Required dimensions/brief elements and applicable accessibility pass; inspected visual result |
| Video/audio/3D | Required export/duration/sync pass, planned inspection coverage completed and limits disclosed |
| Software/mods | Required reproducer/checks/integration and real-user path pass under the project's rules |
| Automation/schedules | Required before/after state or dates verified, effects authorized and ambiguity reconciled |

Qualified human or independent assessment supplies the rubric; missing required modality/checking cannot earn an
acceptance label. Domain-specific task criteria may add stricter requirements; none can remove these floors.

| Claim | Proposed material improvement and decision rule |
| :--- | :--- |
| Light-model quality gain | At least 10 percentage points more accepted outcomes than the same light model directly, within the matched total budget; paired uncertainty excludes zero gain |
| Frontier-model quality gain | At least 5 percentage points more accepted outcomes than matched frontier-direct, or at a binary ceiling at least 0.25 higher mean rubric score with paired uncertainty excluding zero; hard floors unchanged |
| Efficiency gain, not intelligence gain | Observed accepted quality is not lower; paired non-inferiority also passes a predeclared 2-percentage-point uncertainty margin, plus at least 20% lower complete cost or human intervention time; name the improved resource |
| Better than BMAD/Core/Pi on shared scope | Against each eligible current pinned setup, demonstrate either quality improvement of at least 5 percentage points, or the efficiency gate above; all mandatory floors and domain safeguards hold |
| Beyond-software quality gain | Meet the relevant quality/efficiency gate separately for every claimed family against matched direct/tool baselines and any eligible competitor configuration; software wins cannot substitute |

Use paired confidence intervals, with repeated runs grouped by request rather than treated as independent tasks.
For non-inferiority, the lower bound must exceed the negative margin; absence of a significant difference is not
evidence of parity. Choose the uncertainty method before the run and report sample sizes and inconclusive strata.
The uncertainty margin is not permission for an observed quality regression; existing stricter product gates prevail.
Thresholds are hypotheses about meaningful adoption benefit; if infeasible, revise them before a new run, not after
seeing disappointing results. Label an efficiency win as efficiency; do not call it a model becoming more intelligent.

Adoption requires passed integrity/recovery/resource gates and passed family floors on the intended supported
envelope. A light/frontier/beyond-software claim additionally requires its own gate; an overall comparative claim
requires the competitor gate against each named setup. Failure or insufficient samples means unqualified, even if
the old software-only scorecard passes. Report trade-offs instead of asserting dominance when a route loses a resource.

Also report Faber's own consumption and estimated headroom per provider/window, separating it from other people's
account use and lagging vendor counters. Unknown quota attribution stays unknown. A cheap monetary proxy that
exhausts the scarce subscription can be worse in practice; resource trade-offs remain visible alongside cost.
