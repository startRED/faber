# Decisions

The decisions in force: the directions the owner chose and the rules that still change what a person gets. "Owner" means the owner decided; "AI" means an AI agent decided under the owner's delegation; "Owner, details AI" means the owner set the direction and the AI the details. A later decision overrides an earlier one. How each one is built lives in [design/](design/README.md) and [product/](product/README.md). Every other R-number (replaced, finished, absorbed into a page, or about a process that changed) is in [history/decisions.md](history/decisions.md) with its number unchanged; numbers are never reused or renumbered.
