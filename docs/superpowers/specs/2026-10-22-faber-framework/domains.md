# One work method, domain-specific evidence

Status: Flow revision 2 proposal. Examples define acceptance recipes, not newly available product capabilities.

## Shared contract

Every substantial task has an observable outcome, original constraints, permitted scope, suitable inputs/tools,
an acceptance method, a current candidate and a delivery destination. Different domains need different checks.
Do not force a casual conversation into milestones, a birthday card into software tests, or a research report into
an aesthetic vote. Mixed tasks combine contracts and then check the combination.

Separate four questions: does the artifact technically work; are its factual claims supported; does it satisfy
the request; does the person prefer it? One checker cannot substitute for all four. For relevant high-impact
advice, use current authoritative sources, expose uncertainty and retain the appropriate human/expert boundary.

| Work | Useful light-model contribution | Where stronger judgment helps | Evidence and human boundary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Casual explanation, study or tutoring | Retrieval, examples, constrained exercises | Difficult reasoning, ambiguous misconceptions | Checked facts/calculations and learner comprehension; do not certify a wrong answer through a friendly tone |
| Research or decision support | Independent source collection, extraction and comparison | Question framing, conflict resolution, synthesis | Claim/source coverage, date, primary sources, counterevidence; distinguish recommendation from established fact |
| Writing, marketing or translation | Variants, formatting, terminology checks | Positioning, nuanced narrative or difficult language | Audience, constraints, factual checks and human preference; native-language review when project rules require it |
| Documents and presentations | Outline expansion and repetitive edits | Information hierarchy and synthesis | Render actual pages/slides, check missing content, references and overflow; a file's existence is insufficient |
| Spreadsheets and data | Cleanup proposals, structured extraction | Grain, joins, assumptions, causal reasoning | Source integrity, formula recalculation, reconciliation, units and uncertainty; use native calculation |
| Image and interface design | Brief variants, asset metadata and layout scaffolding | Art direction, coherence and trade-offs | Inspect real pixels at target sizes, accessibility, request coverage and taste; a text-only model needs actual vision support |
| Video, audio and 3D | Timing sheets, subtitles, metadata and bounded scene work | Story/pacing, visual/audio continuity | Render/export, duration, sync, clipping, representative samples and actual playback/inspection; creative approval remains separate |
| Software, games and mods | Local transformations, fixtures and bounded implementations | Architecture, hard bugs, unfamiliar interactions | Reproducer, executable checks, integration, real-user path and resources; apply relevant project rules |
| Folder/PC automation | Inventory, reversible grouping and dry-run proposals | Ambiguous classification or recovery choices | Actual effect receipts, before/after checks and recoverability; sending/deletion/payments/publication need authority |
| Personal planning and schedules | Constraint collection and feasible options | Conflicting goals or uncertain trade-offs | Dates, durations, availability and the person's priorities; calendar writes/sending remain distinct authorized effects |

No listed worker capability is assumed from a model name. Routing uses what its connected CLI/tool actually exposes
and what the task-family evaluation has qualified. Without a renderer, vision, source or calculator, the missing
acceptance step stays not run; the framework cannot describe it into existence.

## Example A: sourced purchase comparison

Request: choose equipment meeting a stated budget, compatibility and use case. Capture the original requirements.
The lead identifies the few unresolved constraints. Light workers independently collect current manufacturer specs
and seller terms, preserving links/dates/units; deterministic tools compare totals and compatibility.
A qualified synthesis session handles trade-offs and checks conflicting evidence. A second perspective challenges
the recommendation's decisive assumptions rather than rewriting the same comparison.

Acceptance: every decisive factual claim has a source, totals reconcile, excluded options have reasons and missing
information is explicit. The output is a usable comparison and recommendation. Browsing a shop is not authority
to buy; price freshness and the time of capture are visible. No shopping agent is required for a simple factual answer.

## Example B: a short video from a brief

Capture audience, message, duration, format, supplied assets and taste. A capable session chooses the creative
direction; light workers can prepare a timing sheet, captions and bounded scenes from accepted references.
Use the actually installed media workflow/tools. If styles are genuinely undecided, compare a small number of
reversible representative drafts before rendering the entire video; do not generate ten final videos by default.

Acceptance: inspect the rendered result, verify duration/aspect/sync and caption accuracy, sample every distinct
scene plus all transitions with elevated risk, and listen where audio matters. A sample cannot prove unseen frames
perfect. Person's creative preference is separate from technical validity; publishing requires authority.
Missing licensed assets, voice capability or render tools cannot be replaced by invented paths or a success message.

## Example C: a decision-ready analytical document

Preserve the question and its required inputs. Validate dataset grain, units and source freshness before synthesis.
Use tools for calculations; light workers extract and format bounded sections, while a qualified session handles
assumptions and interpretation. Check that the final document's conclusions follow from the shown evidence.

Acceptance: totals reconcile, cited sources support claims, uncertainty is visible, tables are readable and actual
pages render without missing content. Blind editorial comparison may assess clarity, but it cannot establish that
the analysis is causally valid or the source data is true. Keep source, output and verification linked by version.

## Example D: long-running mixed project

A request for a product launch includes research, a site, a spreadsheet and a video. Keep one accepted outcome
route, with domain-specific subplans in existing conversations. Shared brand claims, dates and prices reference
the same accepted sources. Independent work can proceed concurrently; downstream work waits for its real inputs.

Integration checks verify that numbers, terminology, identity and promised capabilities match across outputs.
When the offer changes, invalidate affected downstream claims/assets rather than rebuilding everything or retaining
stale approval. The person sees deliveries and remaining decisions; live worker detail stays in existing surfaces.

## Domain coverage is a ladder, not a universal guarantee

For each family, record: unsupported, available tooling, candidate produced, technically checked, independently
assessed and accepted by the person where required. Expose only capabilities actually reached. A broad product
can serve new domains through existing tools while being honest about their unqualified checks.

BMAD Builder can define non-code workflows and GSD can be configured with other tools. Compare those actual
configurations where feasible. Do not award Faber an automatic win simply because a competitor markets coding.
The claim to test is higher accepted quality and less supervision on the same task, not a longer list of domains.
