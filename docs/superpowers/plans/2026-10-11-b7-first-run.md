# B7: First Run and Tutorial Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A person who opens Faber for the first time goes through three steps (connected accounts, appearance, first conversation) and, if they say they are new to AI, a short tutorial of coach cards that point at the real screen. Someone who says they already use AI gets two cards for what is new to them. After that Faber stops teaching.

**Architecture:** What is due and what a save contains are plain functions (`ui/setup.ts`, `ui/tour.ts`) that know nothing about the screen. `ui/FirstRun.tsx` holds the stage (a window with no sidebar, a stepper, and the step), step 2 (`LookStep`: three live looks side by side, the first settings, the question about AI) and the pieces `Setup` shares with it. `ui/Setup.tsx` becomes step 1 and takes a `mode` (`firstRun` or `manage`). `ui/Tutorial.tsx` draws the cards over the real screen, which is `inert` meanwhile; each card finds its element through a `data-tour` attribute. The engine only gains two settings, `experience` and `tutorialDone`, merged like every other field.

**Tech Stack:** Rust 2024 (the engine's `Settings`), SolidJS + TypeScript (built by Vite), the Rust test runner `cargo t` (suites `rust`, `ui`, `screen`; the `screen` suite drives the real hidden window over WebView2's DevTools port).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-faber-redesign-design.md`: B7 (all of it), B0 (commit 9, "B7: first run and tutorial"), A13 (the design system, for the tokens the screens use), A14 (Curioso, for the tutorial card), AI-19 and AI-20, and the Review-log lines on first run, `manage` mode, tutorialDone, the "None" AI, coach cards and keyboard use (round 2, items 15, 16 and 18). Erick's decisions (`E:\Documentos\ProjetosIA\faber-notas\decisoes.md`): R18 (ask whether the person already uses AI; experienced people skip the tutorial and see only what is new; beginners get a short, simple one; no teaching after that), R59 (the three themes side by side at first run, with the other first settings) and R75 (the tutorial uses Curioso). The visual design is `E:\Documentos\ProjetosIA\faber-notas\design-final\` (mock s6 and its notes). This is commit 9 of the order in B0. It uses B3's `preview_look` and the error-carrying `save_settings`, B4's `Mark` and `Peek`, and B5b's run button.

## Review Focus

Failure modes the spec implies and a person would meet, most likely first. Each one has a test in the task named after it.

1. **The disk refuses the save on Continue at step 2** (full disk, a read-only folder, an antivirus holding the file). Expected: the step stays open, the error key's sentence shows, nothing advances, no tutorial starts, and the same Continue works once the disk does. Pinned by: Task 6: `first run, step 2: a refused save keeps the step open with the error, and starts no tutorial`.
2. **Reconnecting an account later** (from the no-AI state; from Settings after B8). Expected: it saves `setup` and nothing else, so it never sends the person back through Appearance and never resets `tutorialDone` or `experience`. Pinned by: Task 3: `setup first run: the setup cards save the setup decision and never the appearance, experience or tutorial`. Task 2: `the_first_run_choices_survive_a_patch_that_does_not_mention_them`. Task 6: `reconnecting an account later is a plain dialog and restarts nothing of the first run`.
3. **A narrow window or large text** hides the element a card points at (the Projects button is behind the menu button below 900 px) or leaves no room on the side the card prefers. Expected: the card stays whole inside the window; with its element missing it is centred, with no pointer and no ring. Pinned by: Task 3: `tutorial: a card with no anchor is centred and points at nothing`, `tutorial: a card sits on the first side that has room, with its pointer on the edge that faces the anchor`, `tutorial: when no side has room the card is centred and points at nothing`. Task 6: `first run, tutorial: the card stays inside the window from 720 to 1440 wide`.
4. **Someone who uses only the keyboard, or a screen reader.** Expected: each step's heading takes focus, Esc and the shell's shortcuts do nothing during steps 1 and 2 and during a card (Esc skips the tutorial), Tab never leaves the dialog, the screen behind a card is inert, and focus ends in the composer. Pinned by: Task 6: `first run, keyboard only: ...`, `first run, step 1: ...`, `first run, tutorial: a beginner gets three cards, ...`.
5. **An install from an older version** (its saved appearance cannot be read, or `tutorialDone` is already true). Expected: step 2 shows once, with step 1 shown as done; a person who already finished the tutorial is not taught again. Pinned by: Task 3: `setup first run: step 2 is due exactly when the saved appearance has no base`, `setup first run: Continue sends the choices in one patch, and leaves tutorialDone out when it is already true`. Task 6: `first run: an old appearance with a saved conversation opens at step 2 once, with step 1 done`.
6. **"None" for now.** Expected: no tutorial while no AI is connected (its first card teaches a composer that cannot send), and it starts the first time s1 is shown with an AI. Pinned by: Task 3: `tutorial: it starts only when the first run asked for it, at the new conversation, with an AI connected`. Task 6: `first run: None to the end shows no tutorial, and connecting an account afterwards starts it`.

/** The two steps that come before the first conversation. Step 3 is the new conversation itself. */
export type FirstStep = 'accounts' | 'look';

/** `firstRun` is step 1 of 3 and moves on to step 2. `manage` is only the account cards with a Close button: it saves
 * `setup` and nothing else, so reconnecting an account later can neither restart the first run nor reset the tutorial. */
export type SetupMode = 'firstRun' | 'manage';

/** Step 2 is due until an appearance with a `base` is saved. That covers new users after step 1 and, once, an install
 * that was saved by an older version (whose appearance has no `base`, so the engine hands the screen none). */
export function lookDue(settings: Pick<Settings, 'appearance'> | undefined): boolean {
  return !settings?.appearance?.base;
}

/** The step the window opens at: step 1 under the old rule, else step 2 when it is due, else none. */
export function firstRunStep(settings: Settings | undefined, conversations: readonly unknown[]): FirstStep | undefined {
  if (shouldOpenSetup(settings, conversations)) return 'accounts';
  return lookDue(settings) ? 'look' : undefined;
}

/** What `setup` saves, in either mode. Nothing else about the first run changes with it. */
export function setupPatch(result: 'done' | 'skipped'): Settings {
  return { setup: result };
}

export type SizeChoice = 'small' | 'normal' | 'large';
/** "Normal" is the theme's own size, so it is no saved value at all. */
const SIZES: Record<SizeChoice, number | undefined> = { small: 13, normal: undefined, large: 16 };

/** The appearance with a text size chosen; normal clears the field. */
export function withSize(appearance: Appearance, size: SizeChoice): Appearance {
  const next = { ...appearance };
  const px = SIZES[size];
  if (px === undefined) delete next.textSize;
  else next.textSize = px;
  return next;
}

/** Which "Aa" is on: none when the size was set to something else, such as 14.5 in Settings. */
export function sizeOf(appearance: Appearance): SizeChoice | undefined {
  return (Object.keys(SIZES) as SizeChoice[]).find((k) => SIZES[k] === appearance.textSize);
}

/** What Continue on step 2 saves, in one call. `tutorialDone` is left out when it is already true, so an install that
 * finished the tutorial is not taught again; no answer to the question leaves `experience` out, and counts as a beginner. */
export function continuePatch(appearance: Appearance, experience: Experience | undefined, tutorialDone: boolean | undefined): Settings {
  const patch: Settings = { appearance };
  if (experience) patch.experience = experience;
  if (tutorialDone !== true) patch.tutorialDone = false;
  return patch;
}

import type { Experience } from './api.ts';

// The tutorial's logic, apart from the screen: which cards a person gets, when the tutorial is due, and where a card goes.

/** The card's words are the keys `tour.<id>.title` and `tour.<id>.body`. */
export type TourId = 'chat' | 'projects' | 'follow' | 'auto' | 'running';
export type Side = 'top' | 'bottom' | 'left' | 'right';

export interface TourStep {
  id: TourId;
  /** The value of the `data-tour` attribute on the element the card points at. */
  anchor: string;
  /** Where the card likes to sit, most wanted first. */
  sides: Side[];
}

const CHAT: TourStep = { id: 'chat', anchor: 'composer', sides: ['top', 'bottom'] };
const PROJECTS: TourStep = { id: 'projects', anchor: 'projects', sides: ['right', 'bottom'] };
const FOLLOW: TourStep = { id: 'follow', anchor: 'run', sides: ['bottom', 'left'] };
const AUTO: TourStep = { id: 'auto', anchor: 'auto', sides: ['top', 'left'] };
const RUNNING: TourStep = { id: 'running', anchor: 'run', sides: ['bottom', 'left'] };

/** A beginner gets three cards. An experienced person gets two, for what is new to them: Auto and the running list. No
 * answer to the first-run question counts as a beginner. */
export function tourFor(experience: Experience | undefined): TourStep[] {
  return experience === 'experienced' ? [AUTO, RUNNING] : [CHAT, PROJECTS, FOLLOW];
}

export interface TourState {
  tutorialDone?: boolean;
  /** How many AIs are connected. */
  vendors: number;
  /** The new conversation (s1) is on screen. */
  onNewChat: boolean;
  /** A first-run step, the setup cards or Settings is open. */
  covered: boolean;
  /** The tutorial already ended in this session, whether or not the save of `tutorialDone` worked. */
  endedHere: boolean;
}

/** The tutorial starts when the first run asked for it (`tutorialDone` is false), at the new conversation, with an AI
 * connected, because its first card teaches the composer and that cannot send without one. */
export function tutorialDue(s: TourState): boolean {
  return s.tutorialDone === false && !s.endedHere && !s.covered && s.vendors > 0 && s.onNewChat;
}

export interface Box {
  x: number;
  y: number;
  w: number;
  h: number;
}
export interface Size {
  w: number;
  h: number;
}
export interface Placement {
  x: number;
  y: number;
  /** The edge of the card that has the pointer. None when the card is centred. */
  side?: Side;
  /** Along that edge, from the card's corner, to the middle of the element pointed at. */
  pointer?: number;
}

const clamp = (n: number, low: number, high: number) => Math.min(Math.max(n, low), Math.max(low, high));

/** Where a card of `card` size goes in a window of `view` size. With no anchor (it is missing or hidden), or no side with
 * room for it, the card is centred and points at nothing. A card points with the edge that faces the anchor. */
export function placeCard(anchor: Box | undefined, card: Size, view: Size, sides: readonly Side[], gap = 14, margin = 12): Placement {
  const centred: Placement = { x: Math.round((view.w - card.w) / 2), y: Math.round((view.h - card.h) / 2) };
  if (!anchor) return centred;
  const middle = { x: anchor.x + anchor.w / 2, y: anchor.y + anchor.h / 2 };
  const room = { x: [margin, view.w - card.w - margin], y: [margin, view.h - card.h - margin] } as const;
  for (const side of sides) {
    // `side` is the side of the anchor where the card sits; the pointer is on the opposite edge of the card.
    if (side === 'bottom' || side === 'top') {
      const y = side === 'bottom' ? anchor.y + anchor.h + gap : anchor.y - gap - card.h;
      if (y < margin || y > room.y[1]) continue;
      const x = clamp(middle.x - card.w / 2, room.x[0], room.x[1]);
      return { x: Math.round(x), y: Math.round(y), side: side === 'bottom' ? 'top' : 'bottom', pointer: Math.round(clamp(middle.x - x, 20, card.w - 20)) };
    }
    const x = side === 'right' ? anchor.x + anchor.w + gap : anchor.x - gap - card.w;
    if (x < margin || x > room.x[1]) continue;
    const y = clamp(middle.y - card.h / 2, room.y[0], room.y[1]);
    return { x: Math.round(x), y: Math.round(y), side: side === 'right' ? 'left' : 'right', pointer: Math.round(clamp(middle.y - y, 20, card.h - 20)) };
  }
  return centred;
}

const FOCUSABLE = 'button, [href], input, select, textarea, [tabindex]:not([tabindex="-1"])';

/** Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside an open modal dialog: past its last stop they go back to its first, and the other way. */
export function keepFocusIn(e: KeyboardEvent, dialog: HTMLElement | undefined | null) {
  if (!dialog) return;
  const stops = [...dialog.querySelectorAll<HTMLElement>(FOCUSABLE)].filter(
    (el) => !(el as HTMLButtonElement).disabled && el.getClientRects().length > 0 && getComputedStyle(el).visibility !== 'hidden',
  );
  const first = stops[0];
  const last = stops.at(-1);
  if (!first || !last) return;
  const at = document.activeElement;
  const inside = !!at && dialog.contains(at);
  if (e.shiftKey ? !inside || at === first : !inside || at === last) {
    e.preventDefault();
    (e.shiftKey ? last : first).focus();
  }
}

/** Where the element a card points at is on screen now, or none when it is missing or hidden (the Projects button below
 * 900 px, where the sidebar is behind its button). */
function anchorBox(anchor: string): Box | undefined {
  const el = document.querySelector<HTMLElement>(`[data-tour="${anchor}"]`);
  if (!el || el.getClientRects().length === 0) return undefined;
  const r = el.getBoundingClientRect();
  return r.width > 0 && r.height > 0 ? { x: r.x, y: r.y, w: r.width, h: r.height } : undefined;
}

const RING = 6;

/** One card: Curioso, the step, one or two sentences, and Next and Skip. It is a modal dialog: the screen behind it is
 * inert, focus starts on Next and stays on the card, and Esc skips the whole tutorial. */
function Card(props: { step: TourStep; n: number; total: number; onNext: () => void; onSkip: () => void; onHole: (box: Box | undefined) => void }) {
  const [at, setAt] = createSignal<Placement>();
  let card: HTMLDivElement | undefined;
  let next: HTMLButtonElement | undefined;
  const last = () => props.n === props.total;
  const measure = () => {
    const box = anchorBox(props.step.anchor);
    props.onHole(box);
    const size = card!.getBoundingClientRect();
    setAt(placeCard(box, { w: size.width, h: size.height }, { w: window.innerWidth, h: window.innerHeight }, props.step.sides));
  };
  onMount(() => {
    measure();
    // The card is drawn once it has a place, which is after this batch; a hidden button cannot take focus.
    queueMicrotask(() => next?.focus());
    window.addEventListener('resize', measure);
  });
  onCleanup(() => window.removeEventListener('resize', measure));
  const key = (part: 'title' | 'body') => t(`tour.${props.step.id}.${part}` as Key);
  return (
    <div
      class="coach"
      ref={(el) => (card = el)}
      role="dialog"
      aria-modal="true"
      aria-labelledby="coach-title"
      aria-describedby="coach-body"
      data-side={at()?.side}
      style={{
        left: `${at()?.x ?? 0}px`,
        top: `${at()?.y ?? 0}px`,
        '--pointer': `${at()?.pointer ?? 0}px`,
        visibility: at() ? 'visible' : 'hidden',
      }}
    >
      <div class="coach-head">
        <Mark size={22} />
        <span class="coach-n num">{t('tour.step', { n: props.n, total: props.total })}</span>
      </div>
      <h2 id="coach-title" class="display">
        {key('title')}
      </h2>
      <p id="coach-body">{key('body')}</p>
      <div class="coach-act">
        <button type="button" class="btn primary" ref={(el) => (next = el)} onClick={() => props.onNext()}>
          {last() ? t('tour.finish') : t('tour.next')}
        </button>
        <Show when={!last()}>
          <button type="button" class="btn ghost" onClick={() => props.onSkip()}>
            {t('tour.skip')}
          </button>
        </Show>
      </div>
    </div>
  );
}

/**
 * The tutorial: coach cards that point at the real screen. A beginner gets three, an experienced person two. Finishing
 * or skipping calls `onEnd` once; the screen saves `tutorialDone` and gives focus back.
 */
export function Tutorial(props: { experience?: Experience; onEnd: () => void }) {
  // The cards are chosen once, when the tutorial starts.
  const steps = tourFor(props.experience);
  const [index, setIndex] = createSignal(0);
  const [hole, setHole] = createSignal<Box>();
  let ended = false;
  const end = () => {
    if (ended) return;
    ended = true;
    props.onEnd();
  };
  const next = () => (index() + 1 < steps.length ? setIndex(index() + 1) : end());

  onMount(() => {
    // Capture phase, so the screen's own shortcuts never see these keys: Esc skips, Tab stays on the card, and the
    // shortcuts of the shell (Ctrl N, Ctrl ,, Ctrl .) do nothing.
    const onKey = (e: KeyboardEvent) => {
      const ctrl = e.ctrlKey || e.metaKey;
      if (e.key === 'Escape') {
        e.preventDefault();
        e.stopPropagation();
        end();
      } else if (e.key === 'Tab' && !ctrl && !e.altKey) {
        keepFocusIn(e, document.querySelector('.coach'));
      } else if (ctrl && ['n', ',', '.'].includes(e.key.toLowerCase())) {
        e.preventDefault();
        e.stopPropagation();
      }
    };
    window.addEventListener('keydown', onKey, true);
    onCleanup(() => window.removeEventListener('keydown', onKey, true));
  });

  return (
    <div class="tour">
      <div class="tour-dim" classList={{ clear: !!hole() }} />
      <Show when={hole()}>
        {(box) => (
          <div
            class="tour-ring"
            style={{ left: `${box().x - RING}px`, top: `${box().y - RING}px`, width: `${box().w + RING * 2}px`, height: `${box().h + RING * 2}px` }}
          />
        )}
      </Show>
      {/* A new card for each step, so a screen reader announces it and focus starts on its Next button. */}
      <Show when={index() + 1} keyed>
        {(n) => <Card step={steps[n - 1]!} n={n} total={steps.length} onNext={next} onSkip={end} onHole={setHole} />}
      </Show>
    </div>
  );
}

fn settings(ctx: &Ctx) -> Value {

}

/// Waits for `settings.json` to say `what`; saves go through the engine and reach the disk a moment later.
fn saved(ctx: &Ctx, what: &str, check: impl Fn(&Value) -> bool) -> Result {
    eventually(what, Duration::from_secs(15), || check(&settings(ctx))).map_err(|e| format!("{e}; settings.json holds {}", settings(ctx)))
}

}

/// Presses Tab until the focused element satisfies `test`, an expression over `a`, the active element.
fn tab_to(page: &mut Page, what: &str, test: &str) -> Result {
    for _ in 0..80 {
        if page.eval(&format!("(() => {{ const a = document.activeElement; return !!a && ({test}); }})()"))? == json!(true) {
            return Ok(());
        }
        page.press("Tab")?;
    }
    Err(format!("Tab never reached {what}"))
}

}

fn tutorial_fits_the_window(ctx: &mut Ctx) -> Result {
    ctx.write_settings(&json!({ "language": "en", "setup": "done", "appearance": { "base": "sala" }, "tutorialDone": false }))?;
    let launch = claude_chat(ctx);
    let mut app = ctx.launch(launch)?;
    app.page.wait_visible(&css(".coach"))?;
    let inside = "(() => { const r = document.querySelector('.coach').getBoundingClientRect();                   return r.x >= 0 && r.y >= 0 && r.right <= innerWidth && r.bottom <= innerHeight && r.width > 0; })()";
    for step in 0..3 {
        for (width, height) in [(1440, 900), (1100, 700), (899, 700), (800, 600), (720, 480)] {
            size(&mut app.page, width, height)?;
            app.page.wait_for(&format!("card {} inside the {width}x{height} window", step + 1), inside)?;
            if step == 1 && width < 900 {
                // The sidebar is behind its button, so the Projects button is not there to point at: the card is centred, with no
                // pointer and no ring. The card follows the resize a moment after the window does.
                let centred = "(() => { const c = document.querySelector('.coach'); const r = c.getBoundingClientRect(); \
                       return c.getAttribute('data-side') === null && document.querySelectorAll('.tour-ring').length === 0 \
                         && Math.abs(r.x + r.width / 2 - innerWidth / 2) <= 1.5 && Math.abs(r.y + r.height / 2 - innerHeight / 2) <= 1.5; })()";
                app.page.wait_for(&format!("the Projects card centred, with no pointer, at {width} px"), centred)?;
            }
            if step == 1 && (width == 1100 || width == 720) {
                shot(ctx, &mut app, &format!("b7-tour-2-at-{width}"))?;
            }
        }
        if step < 2 {
            let next = css(".coach .btn.primary");
            app.page.click(&next)?;
            let expected = format!("document.querySelector('.coach .coach-n')?.textContent === '{} of 3'", step + 2);
            app.page.wait_for("the next card", &expected)?;
        }
    }
    ctx.close(app.id)
}

fn refused_save_keeps_step_two(ctx: &mut Ctx) -> Result {
    ctx.write_settings(&json!({ "language": "en", "setup": "done" }))?;
    let launch = claude_chat(ctx);
    let mut app = ctx.launch(launch)?;
    let page = &mut app.page;
    let heading = page.named("heading", "first.look.title")?;
    page.wait_visible(&heading)?;
    choose_experience(&mut app, "first.xp.experienced")?;
    let page = &mut app.page;
    // A folder where the temporary file goes makes every write of settings.json fail while the old file stays valid.
    let blocker = ctx.sb.data.join("settings.json.tmp");
    std::fs::create_dir(&blocker).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let go = page.named("button", "first.continue")?;
    page.click(&go)?;
    let toast = css(".toast[role=alert]");
    page.wait_visible(&toast)?;
    let sentence = page.text_of("error.settings.saveFailed")?;
    ensure(page.inner_text(&toast)?.contains(&sentence), || format!("the alert should say {sentence:?}"))?;
    // The step stays open, can be used again, and nothing was saved or started.
    page.wait_visible(&heading)?;
    page.wait_enabled(&go)?;
    pause(0.5);
    same(page.count(&css(".coach"))?, 0, "no tutorial after a refused save")?;
    let text = std::fs::read_to_string(ctx.sb.data.join("settings.json")).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    ensure(!text.contains("tutorialDone") && !text.contains("experience"), || format!("settings.json should be as it was: {text}"))?;
    // Once the disk works again, the same Continue saves and the tutorial starts.
    std::fs::remove_dir(&blocker).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let page = &mut app.page;
    page.click(&go)?;
    page.wait_gone(&heading)?;
    page.wait_visible(&css(".coach"))?;
    saved(ctx, "step 2 to be saved", |s| s["experience"] == "experienced" && s["tutorialDone"] == false)?;
    ctx.close(app.id)
}

## Deviations from spec

1. **The shell is `inert`, the title bar is not.** The spec says the rest of the app is `inert` while a card is open. The title bar holds the window's own buttons (minimise, maximise, close), which must keep working, so `.shell` is inert and the title bar is not. Esc, Tab and the shortcuts are handled by the card in the capture phase, so nothing behind it reacts.
2. **Ctrl . is blocked as well.** The spec names Ctrl N and Ctrl , for the cards, and "the buttons are the only way on" for the steps. Ctrl . (the running column) is also blocked during both, because it would open a column behind a modal dialog.
3. **Step 1's button.** The spec keeps Skip and "None" and says they move on to step 2. The button that used to say "Done" now says "Continue" (the key `setup.done` is gone, `first.continue` replaces it), and in `manage` mode it says "Close" (`setup.close`). The saved values (`done`, `skipped`) are the same.
4. **A refused save is a scenario, not a `ui` check.** The spec lists "with a rejected `save_settings`, step 2 stays open and the tutorial does not start" under the UI checks. The `ui` checks here run pure functions inside the app and cannot mount the screen, so the pure parts are checks (`continuePatch`, `lookDue`, `tutorialDue`) and the behaviour is a `screen` scenario with a real refused write.
5. **Going Back keeps the choices.** The spec says only "Back". The screen keeps what was chosen on step 2 (the look, the sizes and the answer) while the person is back on step 1, so coming forward again loses nothing. This is held in the screen, not saved.
6. **The cards for "Auto" and the running list point at the model picker and the run button of the new conversation.** The spec says "at the composer's model picker" and "at the run button"; both carry a `data-tour` attribute (`auto`, `run`), and so do the composer (`composer`) and the Projects button (`projects`).
7. **A tutorial card's focus.** Focus goes to Next a moment after the card is drawn (a hidden button cannot take focus, and the card is drawn once it has a place). It is the same result as the spec's "focus moves to Next when a card opens".
8. **Memory scenario.** The spec's M4 ("the first run open on step 2, with no conversation") is a second measurement inside `C5.4`, next to the existing one for step 1, so one scenario reports both.
9. **Test client.** The `screen` suite's keyboard (`testkit/src/cdp.rs`) did not know the Space key, which the keyboard-only scenario needs (spec: "Tab, Shift+Tab, Space, Enter and Esc only"). One line and one unit test add it.
