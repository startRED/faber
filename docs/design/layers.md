# Layers

How the shell moves: what opens or closes is a surface that slides and fades over the canvas, never a track that grows while the stage reflows each frame (the owner, 2026-11-11). The timing tokens and the frequency rule are in [looks.md](looks.md#motion); the widths are in [layout.md](layout.md#arranging-the-shell).

- **One step for the stage.** The grid tracks and the stage never animate (no `grid-template-columns`, `width` or `margin` transition, no FLIP of the stage in `animateList`). When the sidebar or the column opens or closes, the stage takes its final width at once, and the layer that arrives or leaves travels 24 px and fades over `--dur-ui`/`--dur-exit`; the canvas behind a sliding layer is the frame's own colour, so nothing flashes.
- **Peek.** With the sidebar stepped aside (focus mode, or a wide view's fold), a pointer resting 100 ms on the window's left edge (or on the button that leaves focus mode) floats the sidebar over the stage on the look's `--lift-shadow`; the stage keeps its width. It goes 300 ms after the pointer and the focus have left, or on Esc (focus returns to the box). It lives beside the stage only: the narrow overlay has its own button and scrim. Reduced and no motion show and hide it at once.
- **Settings and Catalog are layers.** Settings covers the shell, Catalog covers the stage; what is beneath stays mounted (so scroll, drafts and the open conversation survive) and, once the entrance has covered it, is `visibility: hidden` with its animations paused and `inert`. The entrance is the usual rise-and-fade, the exit the shorter one. The cost is the DOM of the screen under the layer, kept instead of rebuilt; nothing else runs for it, and no `will-change` is set.
- **Views cross-fade.** The running column's views (list, page, changes, file, details, aside, visual) and Catalog's list and a tool's page replace one another through `animateList`: the new one fades in and the old one leaves as an inert copy.
- **One edge.** A layer's edge is the look's `--lift-shadow` ring and shadow, with a transparent border for forced colours; never a border beside a second pasted shadow.

- **Where it is proved.** `tests/ui/shell.check.ts` (`shell: the sidebar steps aside as a layer`, `... resting on the left edge floats it`, `shell: Settings is a layer`, `shell: Catalog is a layer`, `shell: the views of the running column cross-fade`, `shell: the column over the stage has one edge`) and `tests/ui/motion.check.ts` (`closing a column lets the main settle in one step`).
