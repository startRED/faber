# First-user research archive, 2026-10-24

Status: Historical reports preserved verbatim; this page is context, not a current product specification or approval.

This set contains 24 Markdown reports from the first-user study at source `76b0f6bc97d975477e4f9c1ad28381f177812576`. The original queued tree `34fa5ec7c66d12751f07e11d3cc56b8836331772` contains the same 24 file contents. The reports record assisted walkthroughs in Portuguese and English, at two window sizes, in Chat and Projects, plus follow-up, second-pass, synthesis and control observations. Their stated limits and unfinished tasks remain part of the evidence; the reports were restored byte-for-byte.

These records describe that study at that time. They do not establish current behavior, user-wide findings, or a human usability study. A later recorded-chat harness and its safeguards are separate work; preserving the reports does not restore the older restart design.

The reports refer to machine-local screenshots and other artifacts by absolute paths. Those paths may not exist on another computer. The external recovery archive retains 311 distinct image/JSON files that were available at those paths during preservation (34,762,453 bytes); those files are not included in the repository. Their hashes verify the archived current bytes against the source files, not which bytes a historical run loaded, when screenshots were captured, visual quality, or completeness of relative references.
