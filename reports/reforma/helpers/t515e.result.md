limit. It identifies all three P1 blockers as design and native-Git regression requirements:

1. A sibling `.git` can be swapped to a hard link after validation but before Git's truncating repair writes.
   Publication must preserve external sentinel bytes even under that race, with safe parent traversal and ownership.
