## Cost and remaining limits

No new resident process, timer, polling or renderer allocation. Failure retains
existing window, panes and tray for recovery, and the existing shutdown timer
is cleared on rejection. RAM/CPU were not benchmarked; the existing migration
test's synthetic measurement is not a measurement of this shutdown change.

Shutdown has already closed engine input, so a failed attempt preserves shell
access and another Quit but does not resume acceptance of new engine work.
The inherited 60-second policy still requests forced termination of a hung host;
this task does not redesign that policy. Even successful kill must be followed
by confirmed process exit. Failure is reported to the existing shell error log,
without introducing a dialog or a new force-quit option.

Not run: physical crash/power-cut testing, installed-app/manual shutdown,
full Rust/Clippy/screen/memory suites, macOS/Linux execution or the CI landing gate.
