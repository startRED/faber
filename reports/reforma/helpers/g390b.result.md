## Failures and remaining blockers

1. Hard US$25 ceiling: engine/examples/bench.rs:288 gives plain Codex no enforceable in-flight dollar bound, and engine/examples/bench.rs:397 documents completed-boundary ledger accounting and auxiliary overshoot. Existing admission budgets cannot satisfy an absolute ceiling. The watcher splits US$25 into two US$12.50 budgets and passes --require-hard-cap; hard_cap_admission at engine/examples/bench.rs:834 records every skipped row and refuses before CLI discovery/model calls. Both comparisons exit 1 with this blocker. Raising or bypassing the ceiling was not authorized and was not done.
2. Claude quota: bench/account_quiet.rs:56 rejects the app-reader fallback's empty/unverified result. The before invocation exits 1. scripts/bench-when-quiet.mjs:235 suppresses the after invocation and retains unknown-cost admission across retries. Fresh paired quota readings therefore remain blocked.
3. Complete token comparison remains unavailable independently: bench/pure.rs:525 and bench/pure.rs:685 mark routing/auxiliary token counters partial. No claim of no-more-total-tokens can be made from this harness, even after quota and spend blockers are resolved.
4. Native process-start event subscription failed with Windows access denied during preflight. The implemented fallback is a hidden below-normal PowerShell process using Process.GetProcessesByName every 250 ms, without administrator access (scripts/bench-when-quiet.mjs:82). Detection is bounded by polling plus OS scheduling/termination, not an instantaneous event guarantee. No game launch was induced. The real PID-only taskkill command ended the earlier owned watcher tree; game-trigger/retry logic is covered by fake-clock checks, not a live CS2-start test.
