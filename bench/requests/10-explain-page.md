Create rainbow.html in this folder: one page that explains to a 12-year-old how a rainbow forms. Use three short sections, an inline SVG diagram of sunlight entering a raindrop and splitting into colors, and a 3-question quiz at the end that gives instant feedback per answer. It must read well on a phone and on a desktop and use no external files, fonts or libraries.
