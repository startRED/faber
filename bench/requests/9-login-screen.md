Create index.html in this folder: a single-file sign-in screen for a small neighborhood bakery's ordering app. It has email and password fields with visible labels, a show/hide password toggle, a "Sign in" button and a "Forgot password?" link. Validate on submit with inline messages: empty email, invalid email, password shorter than 8 characters. When valid, show a short success message instead of navigating. It must look good and work on a phone and on a desktop, be keyboard accessible, and use no external files, fonts or libraries.
